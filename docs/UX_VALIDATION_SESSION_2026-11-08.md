## Procedure

Use designated test records and the participant's permitted role. Give the task objective without explaining where to click. Start timing when the participant begins; stop when they demonstrate the intended outcome or ask for assistance. Record help, wrong turns, errors and lost work separately. Do not publish policies, submit a real assessment, or alter an existing learner record for a usability exercise.

Proposed targets: 80% of trainers find evidence unaided within 60 seconds; 80% of learners resume correctly within 15 seconds; all pilot participants explain practice versus formal submission before starting a timed attempt.
